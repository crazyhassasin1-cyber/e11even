TRENDING 🔥

Mbappe Faces China Backlash Over Mao Remark

Kylian Mbappe apologises after calling Mao Zedong "some guy - Chinese dictator" in viral meme interview. Chinese brands cut ties, La Liga broadcast suspended.

Real Madrid forward Kylian Mbappe's rapid fall from favour in China followed a familiar pattern: online outrage, corporate distancing and platform restrictions.

Within 24 hours of a podcast clip where he said "some guy - I think he's a Chinese dictator" about a Mao Zedong meme, sponsors Mengniu Dairy, Shanghai Milkground and Eastroc ended cooperation.

"My comment was ill-judged, and I now understand that it hurt many people in China. I apologize to everyone my words have upset." - Mbappe Statement, Tuesday

China Media Group (CMG) suspended all La Liga broadcasts. La Liga president Javier Tebas met China's ambassador saying: "China is and will continue to be a strategic partner for La Liga."

BREAKING TODAY - Oct 28

BREAKING

FLEETWOOD STUNS ARSENAL

In one of the biggest upsets in English football history, fourth-tier Fleetwood Town dumped Premier League champions Arsenal out of the Carabao Cup 5-3 on penalties after a 1-1 draw.

"It's very frustrating to go out. The way we've played was not on the level. We need to do much, much better." - Martin Odegaard

In the shootout, Fleetwood keeper Jay Lynch saved a penalty. Teenager Conor Haughey scored the winning penalty, sending the home crowd wild. 84 places separate these two clubs.
