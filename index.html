BREAKING NEWS

FLEETWOOD STUNS ARSENAL

In one of the biggest upsets in English football history, fourth-tier Fleetwood Town dumped Premier League champions Arsenal out of the Carabao Cup 5-3 on penalties after a 1-1 draw.

The Night Everything Went Wrong

Aaron Loupalo-Bi gave Fleetwood the lead in the first half at Highbury Stadium. Mikel Arteta made nine changes to his side, and the gamble failed. Arsenal struggled badly in the first half.

Captain Martin Odegaard came on in the second half to rescue the game. Viktor Gyokeres equalized after the break, but Arsenal missed several clear chances. To make it worse, 16-year-old wonderkid Max Dowman was sent off with 5 minutes left.

"It's very frustrating to go out. The way we've played was not on the level. We need to do much, much better." - Martin Odegaard

In the shootout, Fleetwood keeper Jay Lynch saved Bruno Guimaraes' penalty. Teenager Conor Haughey scored the winning penalty, sending the home crowd wild. 84 places separate these two clubs in the pyramid.

Other Football News Today

🇪🇸 Spain: Copa del Rey Suspended

The Spanish FA suspended all 24 Copa del Rey matches today following a tragic railway accident in Toledo. Matches will be rescheduled.

🌍 CAF Champions League Draw

Group C is the group of death: RS Berkane, Simba SC, Orlando Pirates, and Colombe Sportive. Draw held in Cairo today.

🇳🇬 Nigeria U-15 Through

Nigeria Future Eagles beat New Caledonia 7-0 to reach Round of 16. They face Benin next.

What Next for Arsenal?

Arsenal must bounce back quickly. They are still top of the Premier League, but this loss hurts. Arteta said he will review his squad rotation. For Fleetwood, this is history. A night they will never forget.
